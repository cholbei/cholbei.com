How information is handled.

This policy describes information handled through cholbei.com and when you contact Cholbei about our services. Client websites and third-party marketplaces have their own privacy arrangements.

Information you provide

When you email us, we receive your email address, the contents of your message, and any details or attachments you choose to provide. Project correspondence may include your name, business information, requirements, and billing or delivery records.

We use this information to respond, prepare quotes, deliver agreed work, provide support, and maintain relevant business records.

Website requests and Resources

Hosting and security providers may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, browser information, requested URLs, and timestamps, to deliver and protect the website. See Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy.

The Resources section sends search terms and item identifiers to Envato through our server-side integration. It caches product results and affiliate links to reduce repeated requests. Avoid entering personal information in resource searches.

Google Analytics and Tag Manager

We use Google Analytics 4 through Google Tag Manager to understand website visits, traffic sources, and interactions. Depending on the active tag and consent settings, Google may use cookies and process information about your browser, device, and activity. Tag Manager manages the tags used on our website. See how Google uses information from partner sites.

Affiliate links and external services

Resources links may pass through Impact before opening Envato. These services may process referral information and use cookies or similar technologies to attribute purchases. Cholbei may receive a commission from qualifying purchases.

External product images are loaded from third-party hosts, which receive the requests needed to display them. When you follow an external link, that provider’s policies apply. Review Envato’s Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy for details.

Access, sharing, and retention

Information may be handled by service providers needed for hosting, email, project collaboration, and agreed delivery. We limit project access to what is needed for the work. Providers may process information in countries other than your own.

We retain correspondence and project records for the purposes described above and any applicable record-keeping requirements. Retention depends on the record and the project; this policy does not promise a single deletion period for all information.

Your choices and requests

You can choose what to include in an enquiry, manage cookies through your browser and third-party settings, and contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information we hold. Requests are considered subject to applicable requirements and any records we need to retain.

Email hello@cholbei.com with the subject “Privacy request”. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

We may update this page as the website or our services change. The date below identifies the latest revision.
